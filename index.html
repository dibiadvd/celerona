⚖️ Material Balance

Estimate hydrocarbons in place from production and pressure data.

🛢️ Undersaturated Oil Reservoir

For reservoirs above bubble point (no gas cap, no water drive).

Effective Compressibility ce—
OOIP (N)—
ce = co + (cw·Swi + cf)/(1-Swi)
N = Np·Bo / (Bo - Boi + Boi·ce·ΔP)

🔥 Gas Reservoir (P/Z Method)

Dry gas reservoir with volumetric depletion.

OGIP (G)—
P/Z = (Pi/Zi) × (1 - Gp/G)
G = Gp / (1 - P/Z ÷ Pi/Zi)

⚠️ Note: The P/Z method assumes volumetric depletion (no water drive). For water-drive gas reservoirs, use the Havlena-Odeh or van Everdingen-Hurst methods.
